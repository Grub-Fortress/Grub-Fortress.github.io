TF:Grub 1 Year Anniversary Post
by Grub, Jul 11th, 2026

Hello, Grub here, today officially marks one whole year since the 0001 build of TF:Grub released, and even if progress on the mod has slowed down quite a bit, I thought it'd be worth it to at least write up a quick blog post
I wish I had prepared something more special, but sadly I didn't, so this post will have to do

When is the new update coming out?
Well, I don't exactly have any specific date, especially since the progress on the update has slowed down, as I've gotten slightly sidetracked with other projects, as much as I'd love to release it today, I don't think this will happen, I'll try to get it out eventually, although I cannot promise anything

Future plans for TF:Grub
I'm honestly quite happy that I've gotten this far with the sourcemod, at least past the point of actually releasing it, considering most of the time whenever I work on something, it ends up abandoned, even if the community is mostly non-existent, I hope that with enough time TF:Grub will turn into something which at least has people playing it from time to time.

As for my future plans, I don't exactly have any specific plans besides hopefully releasing build 0016, then continuing work on the mod, and eventually get the mod out of Open Alpha

and I guess that's about it for the blog post, Have fun Grubbing!
